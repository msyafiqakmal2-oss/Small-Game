  A profile is a structured representation of information about a person, entity, organization, system, or object. It serves as a comprehensive summary that captures the key characteristics, attributes, and data points that define or describe its subject. Profiles exist in virtually every domain of human activity — from social media to medicine, from business to psychology.

   The word profile comes from the Italian word "profilo", which means "to draw in outline." Originally, it referred to a side-view drawing of a person's face — a silhouette that captured the essential features of someone's appearance from a particular angle. Over time, the meaning evolved and expanded far beyond art, becoming a fundamental concept in data, identity, and communication.
